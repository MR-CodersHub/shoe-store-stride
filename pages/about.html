Our story

It started with a pair that hurt.

In 2018, our founder bought a handsome pair of shoes that were half a size too small, because the shop only had that size in the colour he wanted. Six weeks of blisters later, he decided the problem was the system, not his feet.

Stride opened with a single promise we still keep: we will never sell you a shoe we would not size for our own family. That meant learning to measure properly, stocking widths, and standing behind every pair with a 14-day guarantee.

Eight years on, we have scanned over 12,000 feet, repaired 40,000 pairs, and outfitted weddings, run clubs and offices. The store is bigger, but the promise has not changed.

History

Eight years, one promise.

2018

First store opens

A 600 sq ft shop on Khader Nawaz Khan Road with six brands and one rule: size it honestly.

2019

The 14-day guarantee

We started swapping ill-fitting pairs for free — and sales went up. Trust turned out to sell shoes.

2021

Repair bench launched

A care service that has since revived over 40,000 pairs and kept them out of landfill.

2023

3D fitting studio

2025
