Privacy policy.

Your data is your data. This page explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 1 August 2026 · Applies to stride.shop & the store

Stride Footwear Pvt. Ltd. ("Stride", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy describes what we collect through our website, store and services, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect 2. How we use it 3. Cookies 4. Sharing 5. Security 6. Retention 7. Your rights 8. Changes 9. Contact

1. What we collect

We collect the minimum needed to sell you shoes and run our services:

  • Contact details you give us (name, email, phone, address).
  • Order and service history, including sizes and foot-scan data.
  • Payment details — processed by our payment partners; we never store card numbers.
  • Basic device and browsing data (see Cookies below).

2. How we use it

  • To fulfil orders, repairs, trials and bookings.
  • To send order updates and, only with consent, marketing emails.
  • To improve fit — scan data helps us stock the right sizes.
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies

This website uses local storage to remember your cart, theme and layout preferences. We do not use invasive tracking. Advertising cookies are only placed by our ad partners with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

4. Sharing

We share data only with the people who need it to serve you: delivery partners, payment processors, repair couriers, and our store staff. We never sell your personal data. Ever.

5. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS), staff access is role-based and logged, and payment flows are handled by PCI-DSS-compliant partners.

6. Retention

Order records are kept for 7 years (tax law). Foot-scan data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 12 months of account closure. Marketing consent can be withdrawn instantly.

7. Your rights

You can request a copy, correction or deletion of your data at any time. Contact us and we will act within 30 days — usually much sooner.

8. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will notify you by email and update the "last updated" date above.
